Privacy Policy

How the game uses Google sign-in, cloud saves, achievements and local browser storage.

Effective date: 26 October 2026

← Back to SK's Bubble Shooter

1. About this policy

This policy describes information processed when you play SK's Bubble Shooter (the “Game”), particularly if you choose to connect your Google account for cloud saving. You can play without signing in; in that case your progress is stored on your device, subject to your browser's storage settings.

2. Information used by the Game

If you sign in with Google, Firebase Authentication provides the Game with an account identifier and basic profile information that may include your display name, email address and profile photo. The Game uses this information to identify your account and display your sign-in status.

When cloud sync is used, Cloud Firestore stores game-related data linked to your account, such as your level checkpoints, save slots, unlocked achievements and associated game statistics. This is used to restore or sync progress between devices.

3. Local storage and cookies

The Game uses local browser storage, including IndexedDB, for offline game saves and settings. Firebase Authentication uses browser persistence to remember your sign-in where supported. Depending on the browser and authentication flow, cookies or other browser storage may be used during sign-in. The Game does not need advertising cookies to provide cloud saving.

Clearing browser data can remove local saves or sign-in state. Cloud data associated with your account can remain available after you sign in again.

4. Why information is used

  • To sign you in securely with your chosen Google account.
  • To associate cloud saves, levels, achievements and statistics with your account.
  • To restore progress on your other devices and continue offline with local storage.
  • To support account-related functionality, troubleshoot errors and protect game data.

5. Services that process information

The Game uses Google Firebase, including Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, to provide account and cloud-save features. Google's handling of information is also described in the Google Privacy Policy. Data may be processed by Google in accordance with the selected Firebase services, their configuration and applicable terms.

6. Sharing and access

Cloud-save data is intended to be accessible only to the account it belongs to. Firestore security rules restrict access based on a player's authenticated Firebase user ID. The Game does not intentionally publish private save data to other players. Data is shared with service providers only as needed to operate the features described above, or when legally required.

7. How long data is kept

Local game data remains on your device until removed by the Game, your browser, or you. Cloud data can remain associated with your account until it is deleted or the cloud-save service is discontinued. Signing out does not automatically delete your cloud saves.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when Game features or data practices change. The effective date at the top of this page will be updated when changes are published.
